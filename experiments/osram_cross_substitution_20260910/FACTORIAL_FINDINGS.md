# Factorial audit: approximately additive decision scores, not zero interaction

This audit reads the existing 40 NPZs only. No model forward, GPU, training,
checkpoint reselection or CKA was used. The four cells use a single donor, with
Local always preserved:

| Content | Base slot | Active Gap slot |
|---|---|---|
| B | no_gap | move_base_to_gap |
| G | move_gap_to_base | no_base |

The MOSI head produces a raw sentiment regression score, not class logits.
We use `Y = sign(nonzero label) * score`: a signed decision margin consistent with
the current zero-threshold classifier, not calibrated NLL or probabilistic confidence.
Only exactly-one-missing AT/AV/TV samples are included.

## Results

Means weight rates .1–.7 equally within each seed, then seeds 66–70 equally.
There are no eligible samples at rate 0. None of these quantities is a W-F1 point delta.

| Effect | Signed mean | Mean absolute value |
|---|---:|---:|
| B-content minus G-content | +0.037879 | 0.112707 |
| Gap-slot minus Base-slot | +0.005511 | 0.054452 |
| Content × slot interaction | -0.010484 | 0.043189 |

Interaction is **not zero**: mean signs are negative in 30/35 seed/rate groups;
35-group mean absolute interaction ranges from 0.01690 to 0.05757. A small pooled
signed mean would therefore be insufficient evidence by itself.

For effects C, S, I and factor signs c, s in {-1,+1}, exact reconstruction is:

`Y = grand + c*C/2 + s*S/2 + c*s*I/4`.

The interaction-free approximation has a per-cell error of `abs(I)/4`:

- Mean absolute decision-margin approximation error: **0.010797**.
- Mean interaction fraction of within-four-cell factorial energy: **3.64%**.
- Removing interaction changes the decision sign in **0.29%** of the four-cell
  cases on average; individual seed/rate groups range from **0 to 0.733%**.
- These are descriptive diagnostics, not population variance explained, equivalence
  testing, downstream accuracy improvement or an independent-sample significance test.

## Pattern check

The following values average the 35 seed/rate groups within each pattern.

| Pattern | Mean I | Mean abs I | Interaction energy fraction | Additive sign disagreement |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| AT | -0.00823 | 0.03937 | 3.51% | 0.146% |
| AV | +0.00573 | 0.02265 | 1.26% | 0.446% |
| TV | -0.02910 | 0.06758 | 5.99% | 0.286% |

TV has the largest interaction magnitude; AV has the largest decision-sign
sensitivity despite smaller interaction. Do not generalize aggregate magnitude
to identical decision sensitivity across patterns.

## Bounded conclusion

For these frozen checkpoints, selected samples and signed regression scores,
**content and slot main effects provide a good descriptive additive approximation
of the four interventions; a nonzero, pattern-dependent interaction remains**.

This supports the proposed content/slot decomposition at the output-score level.
It does not prove that Base/Gap representations are geometrically independent,
semantically identical, or generated by separable internal mechanisms. The main
effects themselves may include scale and decoder-distribution effects. No
practical-equivalence threshold was fixed in advance, and test utterances recur
across rates and seeds, so no formal equivalence claim is made.
